Customer Churn Analysis | Data Analytics Project
Project Overview
This project analyzes customer churn for a telecommunications company to identify customer behavior patterns, understand churn drivers, and evaluate potential revenue loss.

The objective is to generate actionable business insights that can help improve customer retention.
Tools & Technologies
- SQL: Data exploration, cleaning, analysis and business queries.
- Microsoft Excel: Data preparation and analysis.
- Power BI: Interactive dashboard and data visualization.
Key Business Insights
1. Overall Churn: 26.6% of customers have churned.
2. Contract Risk: Month-to-month contracts account for the highest churn count (approximately 1.7K).
3. Internet Service Risk: Fiber optic customers have the highest churn count (1,297).
4. Tenure Risk: Customers with 0–12 months of tenure have a churn rate of 47.68%.
5. Revenue at Risk: Month-to-month contracts account for ₹121K in monthly revenue at risk.
Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to analyze:
- Customer retention overview
- Churn by contract type
- Churn rate by customer tenure
- Churn by internet service
- Revenue at risk by contract type
Business Recommendations
- Improve retention strategies for month-to-month customers.
- Investigate service-related issues among fiber optic customers.
- Focus on customer engagement during the first 12 months.
- Develop targeted retention campaigns to reduce revenue loss.
Dataset
IBM Telco Customer Churn Dataset.
